Everything you need to cover Apex 1080 and Project Heimdall

Company description

Apex 1080 is a deep technology company building Project Heimdall — the world's first self-contained mixed reality motorcycle helmet with integrated AI, WiFi through-wall human detection, and automated lens cleaning. Founded by engineer and MMA fighter Guillermo Peña, Apex 1080 holds USPTO Provisional Patent Application No. 64/108,615 covering 32 claims. The company is targeting three markets simultaneously: consumer motorcycle riders, military dismounted personnel, and law enforcement tactical units. Prototype bill of materials under $1,500.

Founder bio

Guillermo Peña is the founder and CEO of Apex 1080. A trained engineer, MMA fighter, non-profit co-founder, and author, he has built and shipped multiple technology products including Deal Snatcher (Chrome Web Store) and Mr. Game Player (mrgameplayer.com). He campaigned Apex Rider HUD twice on Indiegogo before founding Apex 1080 to build Project Heimdall — the helmet that sees everything. LinkedIn profile →
